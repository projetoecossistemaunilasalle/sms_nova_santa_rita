#### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The application's conceptual foundation — _"O Caderno de Missões"_ — shines in its `Papel Creme` (`#fffdf6`) and `Tinta Floresta` (`#23402e`) palette, single-corner notched card radii (`--radius-brand`, `--radius-card`, `--radius-feature`), the desktop sidebar's dotted route trail with `Amarelo Marco` active tabs, and its empathetic classroom reassurance copy. However, several implementation drifts dilute this identity:

- Primary portal buttons (`portal.module.css:804-823`) drop the signature `0 5px 0 var(--green-dark)` tactile shadow ("Pressão verde") and `-2px` hover lift defined in `DESIGN.md`, regressing to a generic `0 2px 3px` drop shadow.
- `.shell` (`portal.module.css:1-6`) locally overrides `:root` typography tokens (`--type-compact`, `--type-small`, `--type-nav`, `--type-headline`), flattening headline scale across the portal.
- The public `/jogar` `.entryCard` (`public.module.css:444-459`) omits the two-column tonal art composition described in `DESIGN.md` and applies `border: 7px solid var(--surface)` on `background: var(--surface)` (a white border on a white background).
- `.detailPanel` (`portal.module.css:1077`) applies a resting drop shadow (`0 8px 24px rgb(35 64 46 / 5%)`), breaking `DESIGN.md`'s _Leve em Repouso_ rule.

#### Overall Impression

Missões do Caramelito has a deeply thoughtful product architecture and a warm, purposeful visual direction tailored to municipal schools, shared tablets, and low-friction QR entry. What holds it back right now are classroom edge-case traps (a brief Wi-Fi drop on `/jogar` strips the QR credential from the URL with no retry button; reprinting a lost QR card is hidden behind a search filter while destructive rotation sits on every row), raw database/schema identifiers leaking into teacher results (`when_play_stops_being_play`), and CSS token/cascade bugs that break error indicators, mobile navigation, and tactile button affordances. Fixing the classroom recovery flows and aligning the CSS implementation with `DESIGN.md` will immediately elevate both usability and craft.

- **[P0] Transient `/jogar` Entry Failures Strip `?cred=` From the URL Without Offering a Retry Action, and Waiting Tablets Never Auto-Poll**
  - **Why it matters**: In `src/components/public/player-entry-screen.tsx:85-87`, `window.history.replaceState` strips `?cred=` from the address bar before `resolvePlayerEntryAction` finishes. If a school tablet hits a 2-second Wi-Fi hiccup (`catch` at lines 75–77 -> `state: "unauthorized"`), `.entryProblem` (`lines 218-227`) renders no retry button, and refreshing the page loses the credential completely — forcing the teacher to re-scan tablets across the room. Similarly, tablets on the `"waiting"` screen (`lines 150-176`) never auto-poll when the teacher starts the session.
  - **Fix**: Render a primary `"Tentar novamente"` button in `.entryProblem` whenever `credential.current` is present in memory, and add a `visibilityState === "visible"` + `navigator.onLine` polling interval (every 10s) while `view.state === "waiting"`.
